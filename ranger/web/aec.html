Does Chrome's echo canceller see Web Audio output?

A ten minute answer to the one assumption the browser-microphone plan rests on. Nothing here touches Ranger, the vault, or any file.

Before you press anything

  1. Speakers, not a headset. The whole point is the echo path. On a headset there is nothing to cancel and every number comes out near silence, which looks like success and proves nothing.
  2. Normal speaker volume — the one you use for Jarvis.
  3. Say nothing — until the fourth pass, which counts you down and then asks you to talk over it. Keep talking for the whole four seconds; a pause in the middle measures the pause.
  4. The second button repeats the whole thing with noise suppression on. It is aimed at exactly the kind of room that swings, so it is worth having both numbers.
  5. To answer the second question at the same time, start ranger mic in a terminal first and leave it running. If the capture below works while that is showing levels, PortAudio and Chrome can hold the microphone at once.
